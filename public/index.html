Ready in ten minutes.

No account, no email, no password. Your wallet is the only way in.

  1. Open with your wallet

    Connect and sign one message. The signature becomes your vault key inside your browser. Nothing is sent on-chain and it costs no gas.

  2. Fill it

    Write memories, sort them in folders and fill your self card. You can import your ChatGPT or Claude history; it is unpacked and encrypted on your device.

  3. Connect your apps

    Create an app key, tick the folders it may read and paste it into Claude, Cursor or any MCP app. Apps can suggest memories, you keep or reject them.

  4. Turn on your Signal

    Pick the folders you are happy to make public and choose your link. People ask, your Signal answers as you, and every question lands in your log with its seal.

Plug it into the apps you already use.

The connector is one small file that runs on your computer. It decrypts the folders an app may read, locally, and hands them to the app through MCP.
